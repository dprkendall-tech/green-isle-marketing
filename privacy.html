This page explains how the Green Isle Marketing website handles enquiries.

The enquiry form

The form prepares an email on your device. The website does not automatically send the form, store its contents in a website database or subscribe you to a mailing list. You choose whether to send the draft through your own email app.

If you email us, the name, contact details and project information you provide are received in our business mailbox and used to respond to your enquiry, discuss a proposal and manage any agreed work. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Cookies and hosting

This website does not add advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the website. Your email provider handles any draft or message opened in your email app under its own privacy terms.

Other websites and demos

Portfolio examples may link to Google, Tripadvisor, Facebook and other services. Their privacy terms apply when you visit them. The 79 restaurant proposal uses an email or Facebook handoff for table requests; it does not confirm availability or store reservations.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have sent us, or to request access, correction or deletion, email dan@greenislemarketing.com. Information may need to be retained where required for an agreed project or legal obligations.
