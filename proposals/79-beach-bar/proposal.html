What this proposal delivers

The website

A responsive, one-page experience with real venue imagery, food highlights, photo gallery, independent review link, Google Maps directions and a working reservation-request composer.

The Facebook refresh

A coordinated cover and proposed profile identity, a stronger introduction, directions, booking call to action and three sample posts. The mock-up is separate from Facebook.

How the booking flow works

  1. The guest chooses a date, preferred time, number of guests and seating preference, then enters their name and reply details.
  2. The website validates the date/time against Thailand time and prepares a readable request.
  3. The guest opens a prefilled email to the publicly listed business address, or copies the request and sends it through the existing Facebook page.
  4. The restaurant checks availability and replies. Only that reply can confirm the table.

The website itself stores no booking information and sends nothing automatically. Selecting a time is a preference, not evidence that a table is available. The menu and telephone are not guessed.

For instant live booking: connect the owner’s existing booking provider, or agree table capacity, opening hours, service duration, closure dates, deposit/cancellation rules and an authorised staff notification channel before enabling automatic reservations.

Honest reviews, made easier

The website includes a review invitation available equally to every guest, with links to Google Maps and Tripadvisor. Guests write and submit their own reviews. There is no rating filter, automatic five-star posting or diversion of unhappy customers away from public review sites.

For launch, add the verified business’s direct review-request link from Google Business Profile. Tripadvisor also offers an approved partner review-collection widget; availability and onboarding must be checked before promising an embedded submission form. No review-platform accounts or APIs are connected in this proposal.

Published reviews could later be displayed using an authorised widget or API, subject to provider access and attribution requirements. This is separate from collecting or posting reviews.
