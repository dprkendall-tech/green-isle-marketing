Is the calculator a final quotation?+

No. It uses illustrative package prices and an energy model to show how an instant estimate could work. An installer must confirm their real pricing, survey your property and provide a written quotation.

Will solar work on a cloudy day?+

Solar panels use daylight, so they can generate when it is cloudy, but output falls. Season, shading, roof direction and location all affect generation. A home-specific design is needed to estimate performance.

Do I need a battery?+

No. Solar can work without one. A battery stores surplus generation for later but adds cost and energy losses. Its value depends on your usage, tariffs and system design; a bigger battery is not automatically the better choice.

How are the savings worked out?+

The estimate combines the cost of grid electricity avoided with income from surplus energy exported. It accounts for standing charges and battery losses. It does not model future tariffs, maintenance, finance or equipment replacement. .

Can I get paid for electricity I export?+

An eligible system in Great Britain may qualify for an export tariff, including the Smart Export Guarantee. You must apply to a supplier; payments are not automatic. Northern Ireland has different arrangements. The calculator’s export rate is only a demo assumption.

Is Sunward a real installer?+

Sunward is a concept brand created for this Green Isle Marketing showcase. The image is illustrative, the prices are examples and the enquiry is a demonstration. No installer certifications, testimonials or completed jobs are claimed.

BEHIND THE NUMBERS

An estimate,
with the workings.

These are editable demo assumptions, not live supplier prices, current tariff quotes or an MCS performance calculation.

System
450 W per panel. Base annual generation is system kWp × selected yield. East/west applies 85%; an unknown direction applies 90%. No shade is assumed in manual mode. This portfolio version does not connect to Google Solar or retrieve actual roof data.
Energy demand
Annual bill less a 55p daily standing charge, divided by your selected import tariff. The bill must be for electricity only.
Self-consumption
Daytime usage takes 28%, 38% or 48% of generation depending on your routine, capped at 65% of annual demand. Battery input is limited by surplus, 220 cycles per year and annual demand. Battery output allows 90% efficiency; total supply to your home is capped at 82% of annual demand.
Annual benefit
Self-used electricity × import tariff, plus exported electricity × export tariff. Energy used, exported and lost in storage are counted separately. The monthly bill comparison excludes export income.
Illustrative pricing
8 / 10 / 12 / 16-panel packages: £5,495 / £5,995 / £6,495 / £7,495. Add £2,450 for 5 kWh or £3,950 for 10 kWh. The displayed range rounds to £50 and adds 15% at the top. These are fictional all-in package budgets, not offers.
Limitations
Does not model hourly or seasonal energy flows, future tariff changes, degradation, maintenance, replacements, shading, DNO export limits, financing or unusual access. A full site-specific calculation and written quote are required before making a purchase.
