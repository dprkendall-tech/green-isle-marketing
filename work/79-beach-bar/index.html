79 Beach Bar & Restaurant · Klong Muang, Krabi

Complimentary proposal, prepared for owner review.

This project presents design work and an optimisation plan. The restaurant’s accounts have not been changed, and no marketing performance results are claimed.

Explore the website ↗Read the owner proposal ↗

THE STARTING POINT

Let the real place do the talking.

The beach setting, food and relaxed atmosphere are the strongest story. The proposal brings those details into a clear website, a more recognisable social identity and an easier route to directions, reviews and a table request.

THE WORK

One business. A more connected presence.

  • A responsive website with real venue photography, food highlights and clear directions.
  • A table-request composer with a personal confirmation step from the restaurant.
  • A Facebook cover, proposed profile identity and sample posts using lagoon teal, sunset coral and warm yellow.
  • A public Google Maps audit covering missing links, inconsistent business details, photography and local discovery.
  • A launch plan covering account access, approved content, booking operations and measurement.
View the social proposal ↗Read the Google audit ↗

WHAT COMES NEXT

Approve. Connect. Launch. Learn.

The owner needs to approve the design and business details, clear photography permissions and agree the booking process. The current concept is not an instant booking system. Measurement starts after implementation; design work alone is not evidence of increased bookings.
